Multi-agent decision loop

Attack telemetry is scored by the matching specialist. Each defender proposes an action and utility score. The Decision Engine ranks proposals and forms a joint action set above the shared threshold; those decisions are then resolved against the attacks in the same simulation round. Confirmed blocks, node damage, and record exposure feed the defense report.
